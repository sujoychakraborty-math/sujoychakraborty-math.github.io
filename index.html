Research Interests

My research primarily deals with the study of parabolic vector bundles and parabolic Higgs bundles and their moduli spaces, and their correspondence with vector bundles on root stacks. The notion of parabolic bundles was introduced by C. S. Seshadri and V. B. Mehta to extend the theorem of Narasimhan and Seshadri to the case of Riemann surfaces with given punctures. Since its introduction, the notion of parabolic bundles and their moduli have seen applications in addressing many problems, for example to obtain a desingularization of the moduli space of usual semistable vector bundles of rank two and degree zero. Parabolic Higgs bundles (parabolic bundles together with Higgs field) make appearance in the mirror symmetry related problems introduced by Strominger-Yau-Zaslow, which have found its importance not only in the field of mathematics, but in string theory as well.
